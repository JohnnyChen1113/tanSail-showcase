# Code of Conduct

TanSail is committed to a welcoming, constructive, and harassment-free community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific when giving feedback.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, documentation, and observable behavior.
- Welcome different experience levels and help contributors understand project constraints.
- Credit other people's work and disclose relevant sources, licenses, and conflicts of interest.
- Respect privacy and remove credentials or personal information from public reports.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate
disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Repeatedly
pressuring contributors to ignore licensing, security, or clean-room requirements is also
unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, release channels,
and other spaces where someone represents the TanSail community.

## Reporting and enforcement

For immediate security concerns, use a private GitHub security advisory. For conduct concerns,
contact the repository owner privately through the contact methods on their GitHub profile. Do not
open a public issue containing sensitive personal information.

Maintainers will review reports in good faith, protect reporter privacy where possible, and take
proportionate action. Responses may include a private warning, content removal, temporary
participation limits, or a permanent ban. Maintainers must recuse themselves when they are directly
involved in a report.

Good-faith reports and respectful disagreement will not result in retaliation.
